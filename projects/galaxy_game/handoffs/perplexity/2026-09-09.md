  - Deposits should be modeled as **multi-mineral assemblages** (e.g., hematite + magnetite + goethite on Mars; ilmenite-dominant regolith on Luna).  
  - Geosphere JSON is a **guide/constraint**, not an exhaustive map; the spawner adds controlled randomness within those constraints.

- **Three-layer view architecture**:
  - Reviewed existing spec: `docs/new_agent/projects/galaxy_game/architecture/three_layer_views.md`.

    - Surface and TerrainForge share one `terrain_data` and rendering pipeline; TerrainForge is just a zoomed camera state.  
    - Planetary has separate macro data (atmosphere, weather, global biome heatmap).  
  - Identified need for a **research-only data-contract audit** to verify what’s actually implemented vs. specified, to inform future wiki work.
