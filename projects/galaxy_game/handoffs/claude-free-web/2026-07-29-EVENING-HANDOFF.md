## New standing facts from this session

- MVP ISRU processing chain (relevant to any pile-based work): TEU takes raw_regolith → produces processed regolith (pile) + volatiles (tanks); PVE takes processed regolith from that pile → produces depleted regolith + mostly O2 (tanks). Regolith legitimately sits in piles between stages — persisting pile state between processing units is correct, not a bug.
- Material piles have no owner column on the model itself; ownership (whichever corp/player) is conceptually meant to also show up in that owner's inventory, but for settlement-level availability counting, ownership doesn't matter — only the settlement total. The same material may legitimately exist in both `items` and `material_piles` simultaneously.
- Piles vs. inventory items, architecturally: piles exist so raw/intermediate materials (e.g. regolith) can be processed without environmental controls, since they wouldn't fare well being brought inside; only finished goods move into interior inventory/storage.
- Orbital structures are meaningfully different from surface settlement buildings: surface settlements assume implicit internal transport between storage and landing pad (no location check needed); orbital structures are physically disconnected and don't get that assumption for free. This is the core design distinction behind the marketplace/cargo-logistics reframe.
